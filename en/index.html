YOUR QUESTIONS

Practical answers.
Made simple.

Who is La Qualia for?

Veterinary clinics and hospitals that want to improve onboarding, training and day-to-day support for their employees, while making managers’ work easier. Solutions are tailored to the roles, needs and practices of each clinic.

What can custom training include?

Interactive training built from your knowledge and internal procedures, tailored to your clinic’s needs: work processes, customer service, how emergencies are handled and much more.

Each course can include quizzes, scenarios and interactive activities, with statistical tracking that lets managers follow employees’ progress and what they have learned.

Do we have to change our work tools?

La Qualia favours integration into your existing environment. Technical options and constraints are assessed before deployment to keep new habits to a minimum.

How can artificial intelligence help a veterinary clinic?

Assistants that use artificial intelligence give access to your clinic’s information and procedures when your teams need them, for example to find an internal procedure. They draw on your own content and do not replace human follow-up, which remains a core part of the method: listening to employees, analyzing needs and making adjustments with managers.

How can we support employee retention in a veterinary clinic?

By supporting onboarding, skills development and engagement: a training path from day one, resources that are accessible every day, and human follow-up that gathers employee feedback so support can be adjusted with managers. These benefits are intended outcomes and are tracked according to your clinic’s reality.

How can La Qualia help us attract new employees?

A clinic that invests in its employees’ development also becomes more attractive to future candidates. By offering structured onboarding, interactive training and tools that are accessible every day, you show candidates that they will be able to learn, grow and feel supported from the day they arrive. A concrete advantage for standing out as an employer and encouraging your teams to stay.

How does La Qualia keep our data secure and comply with Quebec’s Law 25?

Protecting your information is a priority. Each solution is designed with your digital environment, authorized access and the requirements of Law 25 in mind. We favour using your existing tools and limit the handling of personal and client information to what is strictly necessary. Security measures and responsibilities are defined with your clinic before deployment.

How does an engagement get started?

A conversation helps us understand your needs, roles and existing resources. The components to deploy are then defined according to your reality, with follow-up once they are in place.
